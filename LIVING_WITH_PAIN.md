# On Living With Pain

Life is wonderful, but the opportunities of the body and mind, come with the inevitable mistakes that cause use pain.  These pains might be psychological or mental.  They might be temporary or permanent. Important to any of these situations is a reminder of how the body works. In any kind of pain there are two aspects: the physical arousal caused by something that sparks pain and our judgement of that arousal.

There are indeed things in life which we may not be able to control cause us pain. Ask any older person! It is important to remember that one can live with pain (though not ideal) and still pursue ones values. Living with pain might also come with the subconcious rising ideas that are negative as well. Though not easy, it is important to realize that subconcious ideas offered by our brain don't have to be entertained. Treat your body and subconcious gently. These are systems evolved into us that try to keep us safe.  If your pain cannot be avoided and you must continue on with your values anyways, treat these systems as false alarams, which annoying, can be treated as unimportant as they are.

Obviously, pain if possible for our values, should be avoided.  And if the pain is complex, it should be researched (in time boxed amounts that don't overtake our life) to ameliorate if possible.  Remember above all, that living life to acomplish values no matter what state you are in is most important.

Some sources i've found valueable related to the ideas above:
* Ayn Rand's view of the subconcious
* Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for pain which is caused by irrational beliefs that can be corrected
* Acceptance Commitement Therapy for pain which cannot be controlled, but nevertheless one must live
* Stoic writings in general and their rational understanding of trying not to be bothered by that which cannot be controlled
