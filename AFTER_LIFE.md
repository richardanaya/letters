# On After Life

Out life and values is the most important thing that we should be concerned about until it's very end. I write this letter to give you the summary of all I have thought about after life.  There is very little that can be known, but what I do know I hope can bring some comfort and clarity.  The first and most important thought is that our conciousness belongs to existence, and existence is eternal.  Extistence does not arise from nothingness, nor can existence turn into nothingness.

The implications of this have been profound to me, recognizing that before my concious existed, I was a part of the system of existence, and after my conciousness continues to sustain itself, "I" (specifically my components anyways), will return to that system. I find this conclusion fascinating in that we are forever a part of a system that has the potentiality to give rise to conciousness.

I do not believe in re-incarnation, but I believe the conclusion of the above indicates some system of ever appearing conciousness that our components are always a part of. It does not mean we should not squander our life, in fact this is the only individual life as we are we will have, so make the most of it!  Conciousness is a fantastical part of existence,  a part of existinence knowing itself and experiencing the joys of things that sustain itself. I wish you the very best life whoever reads this.

If one knows they are approaching the end, one suggestion I have is to focus on witnessing or helping your loves ones and friends thriving. Through rational love for these people, it's a pleasure I think to know the values you admired in them continue forward.

Note: it's important to emphasize that I do not believe in a detached soul from our body, nor do I believe in determinism of our conciousness from our body as atoms. Nor do I believe in some view that conciousness exists in all tiny things. Concioussness and it's free will exists (axiomatically), and is a question for science to understand its depth.
