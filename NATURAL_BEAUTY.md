# On Natural Beauty

While it is true, that the man-made is of most importance in this world, reason applied to reality in service of our values.  One should not ignore the benefits of Natural beauty. Our senses are evolved to see the percepts of nature, and certain patterns beautiful in the man made are often reflected in the natural. In the acceptance of the identity of being a human, we should recognize the pleasure and rest that comes from the natural experience. Of all the masters of this craft, I attribute japanese gardens at their hight.  While full of natural specimens, they are entirely unnatural arrangement. They have perfected the ability to emphasize aspects of nature that are pleasant to the senses. While this isn't to say that nature in it's raw can provide nothing to the eye.  We often will find that those places most beautiful are beauty accessible only because of the access provided by man.
